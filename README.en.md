# Demonward Panda 64: Self Explorer V4

A quiet space to explore your everyday preferences, in Chinese or natural, everyday English. Use **中文 / English** at the top of the page. Your language choice stays in this browser. A link ending in `?lang=en` opens English directly.

Start with 24 questions. Areas with enough answered questions and exactly tied scores get two follow-ups each, for one round. The first section has 24–36 questions. Then view your result or choose to finish all 60. Questions are shuffled, don't repeat within an exploration, and keep their order when you resume.

Each question has three scored choices and a fourth, unscored exit. The middle choice means the two sides genuinely fit about equally, or you have no clear preference—not that you cannot judge the situation. Optional notes are saved with Next. Note text doesn't automatically change quiz scores.

**Save result image** makes a PNG on your device and requests a download. A preview lets you save it again or press and hold / right-click the image. It includes scores and up to three pairs of possible strengths and things to keep in mind, selected from areas with a clearer direction and enough scored responses. Tied, near-midpoint, and under-answered areas do not become one-sided trait claims. Recovery codes, individual responses, and your own notes stay out of the image.

Changing language keeps answers, the current question, notes, and unfinished form entries. Original scores and IDs stay the same. English is localized wording, not evidence that measurement equivalence across languages has been validated.

Six exploration areas: company/time alone, how you take in information, where you start a decision, how you make plans, how you start something new, and how you show care. A/O and H/C are this site's own extensions. Neither side is better. Percentages describe scores from these questions, not the chance that you are a certain type.

You can use local saving only. Automatic completion summaries, private recovery, and saving note text have separate consent choices. See [About your data](PRIVACY.en.md). A recovery code is a private key to an archive; keep it safe.

This beta is for fun and self-reflection. It is not a diagnosis, a validated clinical instrument, or a basis for high-impact decisions. Question counts and the presence or absence of ties do not establish accuracy.
