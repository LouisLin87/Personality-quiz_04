# Self Explorer V4: English questionnaire

English copy: plain-en-1.0. All 60 questions use the same IDs, option values, and scoring directions as the revised Chinese beta.9.4 questionnaire. This is a localized beta, not a claim of validated measurement equivalence.

Choose what you're usually like. The middle answer means the sides fit about equally or you have no clear preference. If the situation doesn't apply or you can't tell, choose the fourth answer, which isn't scored. Optional notes don't automatically add to quiz scores.

Start with 24 questions. An area with enough answers but a 50/50 score gets two follow-ups, once. Then choose to stop or continue to all 60; questions don't repeat.
